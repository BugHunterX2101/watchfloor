What Watchfloor is

Watchfloor is a demonstration Alexa+ add-on built for the Amazon Alexa+ and AWS hackathon. It reports risk in a fictional sales pipeline and, when you confirm, creates tasks, alerts and notes in that pipeline. By linking or using it you agree to these terms.

Use it for the demonstration only

  • Do not enter real customer, personal or confidential information. The workspace is fictional, and some of what you say is processed by third-party AI services (see the privacy policy).
  • Keep your Watchfloor sign-in to yourself, and do not try to reach other accounts' data, overload the service or get around its limits.

Answers can be wrong

Risk scores follow fixed rules, but spoken answers, deep reviews and drafts are generated by AI models and can be incomplete or wrong. They are not business, financial or legal advice. Nothing changes in the pipeline until you confirm it, and you are responsible for what you confirm.
